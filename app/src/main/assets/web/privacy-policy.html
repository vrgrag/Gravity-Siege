Privacy Policy

Gravity Siege · Effective Date: August 2026

Official page: https://gravitysiegge.com/privacy-policy.html

This Privacy Policy describes how Gravity Siege ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Gravity Siege mobile application ("Service").

Information We Collect

The Service may store limited information on your device that is needed to run the game:

  • Coin balance, bet preference, crew and daily drop progress
  • Sound setting
  • Basic device technical data if the operating system provides it for crash logs

We do not ask for your name, email, payment cards, or government identification. Coins stay on your device and cannot be bought, sold, or exchanged.

How We Use Information

  • Save your progress and settings on this device
  • Keep the game stable
  • Respond to support requests that you send us

Data Storage

Game progress is saved locally on your device. Uninstalling the app removes this data. We do not operate an account system and do not sell personal information.

Children

The Service is intended for users who are at least 18 years old.
